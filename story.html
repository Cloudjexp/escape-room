Chapter One

🏰 The Haunted Castle

Ravenswood Castle has stood empty on the hill for three hundred years, ever since its last lord, Alaric Blackwood, vanished on the night of a terrible storm.

Villagers say the castle is haunted. Lights flicker in the highest tower, and a cold voice whispers through the empty halls after midnight.

Tonight, you dare to enter the castle gates, armed with nothing but your wits and your knowledge of English.

Legend says five ancient guardians protect the truth about what happened to Lord Blackwood: a raven, a crown, a tower, a shield, and a ghost.

Each guardian will only reveal its secret code to someone clever enough to pass its test: vocabulary, grammar, reading, logic, and one final riddle.

Collect all five codes, open the ancient chest hidden in the crypt, and uncover the castle's darkest secret. If you dare... enter now.

📜 How to play

  • ✔ Solve the five challenges in any order.
  • ✔ Each challenge gives you one secret code — write it down!
  • ✔ Your progress is saved automatically, so you can leave and come back.
  • ✔ Enter all five codes on the Final page to open the ancient chest.
